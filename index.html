How to Play

AimMove the mouse left / right (or A / D, ← / →).
PuttClick and hold, drag the mouse back (toward you) to set power, release to putt. Drag forward again or right-click to cancel.
LookScroll to zoom. Press V to toggle an overview of the hole.
RulesWater, lava and out of bounds cost one stroke and the ball returns to where you played from. Each hole is capped at 10 strokes.
KeysR restart hole · M mute · Esc / P pause · Space skip flyover (or hold Space to charge a putt).
HolesPick any hole from “Choose a Hole” on the main menu, or pause mid-game and use “Jump to Hole…”.
ScoresYour best round and best score on each hole are saved in this browser.
